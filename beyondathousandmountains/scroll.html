《千山獨行》第一部古詩九十八首，一詩一圖。左頁為詩意圖，右頁為竪排全詩與小序，鈐作者「其志」印；有英譯者附英文頁。點選任一幅，可左右翻閱。

All ninety-eight classical poems of Part One of Beyond a Thousand Mountains, one painting to each poem: the painting on the left leaf, the full poem and preface in vertical script on the right, sealed with the poet’s seal 「其志」, with an English leaf where a final translation exists.
