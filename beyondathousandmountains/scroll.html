寫意

Xieyi — One Painting to Each Poem

依臺灣中華書局版《千山獨行》之部、篇次第，一詩一圖。第一部《千山獨行》古詩九十八首；第二部《國風入懷》今錄一百三十八首。左頁為詩意圖，右頁為竪排全詩與小序，鈐作者「其志」印；有英譯者附英文頁。點選任一幅，可左右翻閱。

Following the parts and sections of the Chung Hwa Book Co. edition of Beyond a Thousand Mountains, one painting to each poem: 98 poems of Part One, and 138 poems of Part Two, Airs of the States Within. The painting is on the left leaf; the full poem and preface in vertical script on the right, sealed with the poet’s seal 「其志」, with an English leaf where a final translation exists.
